Research Methodology

Evidence-First Research

Research results are designed around reproducibility. Relevant work may preserve exact inputs, hashes, canonical decoded fields, expected values, observed values, execution results, logs, reproduction commands, and invariant results.

Security conclusions are not inferred from isolated or synthetic observations. Findings are intended to be independently reproducible before being treated as security-relevant results.

Open Research

Public research repositories are maintained to make methodology, documentation, reproducible test cases, and selected research material available for independent review.
