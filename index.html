Research Methodology

A structured approach designed to make technical observations reproducible and independently reviewable.

01

Data

Define the research scope, identify the relevant blockchain data, and document the data collection and processing procedures.

02

Analysis

Apply structural, statistical, and differential analysis to identify measurable patterns or differences in observed behavior.

03

Verification

Reproduce technical observations and compare results against independently obtainable data where applicable.

04

Evidence

Document observations, procedures, limitations, supporting material, and the distinction between technical evidence and interpretation.

Evidence Principles

The research presentation emphasizes technical transparency and avoids conclusions that exceed the available evidence.

Reproducibility: Methods and research scope should be documented so that relevant observations can be independently examined.

Verification: Where possible, technical observations are checked against independently obtainable blockchain data.

Separation of claims: Observed technical behavior is distinguished from interpretation, causation, and attribution.

Limitations: Research conclusions remain bounded by the available data, methodology, and verification scope.
