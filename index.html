About the Researcher

Qahtan Al Oud is an independent security researcher specializing in Bitcoin and blockchain forensics, digital investigation, and historical blockchain research.

His work focuses on reproducible technical evidence, statistical analysis, blockchain data examination, and differential testing of consensus-critical execution paths.

Research is presented with an emphasis on methodological transparency, independent verification, and clear separation between technical observations, interpretations, and attribution.

Research Methodology

A research approach centered on reproducibility and independently verifiable technical evidence.

01 · Data

Collection and examination of historical blockchain data with documented scope and processing procedures.

02 · Analysis

Statistical, structural, and differential analysis of observed blockchain behavior.

03 · Verification

Reproduction and independent verification of technical observations where applicable.

04 · Evidence

Clear documentation of observations, methodology, limitations, and supporting technical evidence.
