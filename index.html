Evidence Framework

Evidence-first methodology

  • Source data
    Historical blockchain records and associated research artifacts.
  • Reproduction
    Procedures are structured so that an independent researcher can inspect the methodology and outputs.
  • Statistical analysis
    Quantitative tests may be used to evaluate observed patterns and deviations.
  • Differential testing
    Consensus-critical execution paths can be compared across implementations or controlled test conditions.
  • Evidence separation
    Observations, analytical results, and attribution hypotheses are kept conceptually separate.
