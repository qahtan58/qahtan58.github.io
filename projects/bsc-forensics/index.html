BNB Smart Chain research

BSC-Forensics

Historical consensus and state-transition differential testing framework.

An open-source research and developer-infrastructure project for reproducible analysis of historical BNB Smart Chain behavior.

Research scope

  • Historical blockchain data analysis.
  • Consensus-related behavior and state-transition execution.
  • Differential testing and structural inspection.
  • Statistical analysis, evidence preservation and reproducible workflows.

Methodology and limitations

The repository separates source data, validation procedures, analytical methods, observations and interpretation. Statistical or differential-testing results do not independently establish causation, intent, authorship or attribution. The scope and status of each experiment must be read from its own artifact.
