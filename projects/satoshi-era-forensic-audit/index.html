F28 — CBigNum script matching analysis

8,713 exact byte-level matches were reported among 9,993 unique observed coinbase push[1] payloads, when compared with the historical CBigNum getvch() serialization model over integers 1–199,999 inclusive.

This is a computational matching result under the stated model and range. The match count alone does not establish that every matched payload was historically an ExtraNonce or that CBigNum was the source of every match.
